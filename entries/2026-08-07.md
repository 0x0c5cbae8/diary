Dear diary,

Today felt like day one, even though I've technically been here for a couple of days now getting settled into Fleming. The energy on campus is already palpable, even before orientation officially kicks off. Everyone I’ve met so far is incredibly smart, almost intimidatingly so, but also really friendly and just buzzing with ideas.

I spent most of the afternoon trying to get my room organized. You wouldn't believe how much gear I brought, thinking I'd need every textbook and circuit component imaginable. Now my desk is a controlled chaos of books, a half-assembled Raspberry Pi project (a habit I need to curb if I'm going to survive classes), and the remains of a truly questionable dining hall sandwich.

Later, I was walking past Millikan Library, looking up at its sheer verticality, and thinking about how much I still have to learn. It’s like standing at the base of a really tall mast, with all these complex systems and disciplines stretching upwards. And then, for some reason, the word 'topsail' just popped into my head.

It’s such an old nautical term, but it felt right. Caltech feels like a vast, new ocean, and I’m just a tiny vessel setting out. But it's also about setting a topsail – reaching for that next, higher level of knowledge, catching the wind of innovation and discovery to propel myself forward. It's exhilarating, a little terrifying, but mostly exhilarating. I’m here to learn to design those 'topsails' of human ingenuity, to reach higher than I ever thought possible.

I've got a meeting with my Peer Academic Advisor tomorrow morning, and then a mandatory 'getting to know your house' event in the evening. Hopefully, I can find some fellow space enthusiasts or at least someone who appreciates a good theoretical physics debate.

Wish me luck, diary. The journey has truly begun.

-Trevor
