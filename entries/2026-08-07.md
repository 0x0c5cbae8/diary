Dear diary,

You start to question if you're pulling your weight, or if you're just happy to be along for the ride.

The sheer complexity, the ingenious way they harnessed natural forces.

And then it hit me – the image of a ship at full sail, pushing through the waves. And the *topsail*. It's not just another sail; it's the one set high up, catching the wind that might be stronger or steadier than what's closer to the deck. It's about reaching higher, optimizing, making the most of every available force to propel forward.

That's what this place feels like, isn't it? Like we're all here, just starting out, learning the ropes (literally, in some labs, I hear!). We're figuring out how to navigate these uncharted academic waters, but the goal is to hoist our own *topsails*. To find that unique wind, that higher purpose, that challenging problem that will take us further and faster than we ever thought possible. It’s not just about getting a degree; it’s about becoming the *topsails* of our generation, driving innovation forward.

Okay, maybe I'm overthinking it. It's Caltech, I guess that's what we do here. For now, I'm just going to try to catch a few strong intellectual winds this weekend and maybe, just maybe, figure out how the laundry machines work in Dabney.
