Dear diary,

Wow. It’s only Sunday, but this entire week feels like a month. Frosh Week is… something else. My brain feels like it’s been run through a particle accelerator, then reassembled by a toddler. Mostly in a good way, I think.

I spent most of yesterday in a daze, just trying to process all the new faces, the names of obscure campus buildings, and the sheer volume of "advice" we're getting. Every conversation is a rapid-fire exchange of majors, hometowns, and what obscure scientific concept someone is currently obsessed with. I feel like I'm constantly running an internal algorithm, trying to *detect* the patterns, the unspoken rules, the specific brand of Caltech eccentricity that seems to permeate everything. Am I supposed to be this overwhelmed? Is everyone else just better at hiding it? My social *detection* apparatus is definitely getting a workout.

But then today, during a House brunch (which was surprisingly decent – I've already *detected* the best waffle station), one of the upperclassmen, a senior in Astrophysics, was talking about her SURF project from this summer. She was working on refining techniques for the *detection* of exoplanet atmospheres using spectroscopic analysis. She went into detail about signal-to-noise ratios, false positives, and how difficult it is to *detect* a faint chemical signature from light-years away when you’re battling instrument noise and stellar interference.

It hit me, listening to her, how profound the concept of "detection" is here. It’s not just about finding something; it's about discerning a signal from the noise, about confirming what’s real amidst a sea of potential errors. And I realized that’s what I'm doing in my own way right now, too. I'm trying to *detect* my place in this new, incredibly brilliant, and slightly bewildering environment. I'm trying to *detect* the core of what Caltech will be for me, amidst the initial chaos. Am I picking up the right signals? Am I filtering out the noise of my own anxieties?

It's both daunting and incredibly exciting. To be surrounded by people who are literally building the tools to *detect* the universe's deepest secrets, while I'm here trying to *detect* which dining hall has the best coffee. Maybe one day my detection challenges will involve less caffeine and more cosmic dust. For now, I'll just try to *detect* where I left my laundry card.

-Trevor
