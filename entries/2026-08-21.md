Dear diary,

Wow. Just… wow. It's only been a few days, but my brain feels like it’s been put through a quantum accelerator. Orientation Week is no joke. I’ve probably learned fifty new names, navigated the entire campus twice, and been told about enough academic resources to fill a small library. Everyone here is so ridiculously smart and passionate, it's inspiring and a little intimidating all at once. I keep wondering if I really belong, or if my acceptance letter was a cosmic fluke.

I’ve been trying really hard to put myself out there, going to every social mixer, every "get to know your House" event. I’ve had tons of surface-level conversations – "Where are you from?", "What's your major?", "Did you understand what that professor was talking about in the 'intro to intro to research' seminar?" – but nothing has really… *clicked* yet. It feels like I’m constantly hitting refresh on a webpage that just won’t load completely.

Then tonight, at the Ricketts House pizza night, I ended up talking to this guy, Maya (yeah, I know, but it suits him), who's apparently a huge fan of vintage synthwave. We started talking about obscure analog synthesizers and the physics of sound waves, and suddenly, it was like a circuit completed. Everything just *clicked*. We weren't just exchanging pleasantries; we were genuinely connecting over something we both geeked out about. We debated the merits of wavetable synthesis versus subtractive synthesis for what felt like an hour, completely oblivious to the chaos around us. It was such a relief to feel that connection, that easy flow of conversation.

It’s funny, earlier today, I was trying to get my new external monitor to connect to my laptop, and the DisplayPort cable just wasn't quite seating right. I pushed it in a bit harder, heard that satisfying little *click*, and suddenly the screen lit up. I guess I'm hoping my whole Caltech experience will be a bit like that – maybe a bit of a struggle at first, but then something will just *click* into place, and everything will make sense, or at least feel right.

Tonight’s conversation with Maya gave me a glimpse of that. It made me feel like maybe I *will* find my people here, and maybe, just maybe, I belong after all.

-Trevor
