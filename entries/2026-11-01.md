Dear diary,

November already. Where did October go? Probably into the void where my sleep schedule used to reside. Sarah was deep into debugging some Python code for CS 1, mumbling to herself about recursion, while Liam was pacing, muttering differential equations for Ph 1a. I was just trying to grasp the next chapter in my calculus textbook, occasionally glancing up at their intense focus.

It’s weird, you know? Back home, the idea of living in such close quarters, sharing every creak and groan, would have been intimidating. And when Sarah finally let out a whoop because her code compiled after three hours, it was a tiny victory for all of us. Even managed to coordinate a plan for a suite-wide pizza order tonight. Small wins, small wins. Guess I should probably clear my desk for the pizza.
