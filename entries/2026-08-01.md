That's a *characteristic* I'm quickly picking up on – the ability to find intellectual fascination in absolutely everything.

The intricate gates, the older buildings hinting at decades of groundbreaking research, and yes, the occasional rubber duck hiding in a bush, a nod to some past prank or inside joke. It’s a place that fosters serious intellect but also a very distinct, quirky culture.

The most challenging part, I think, is trying to figure out if *I* possess all the "right" characteristics, or if I'm just faking it until I make it. Will I develop that signature Caltech drive, that comfort with constantly being challenged, that unique blend of academic rigor and playful mischief? It's a bit overwhelming to think about molding myself into this new environment, but also exciting. I hope my own characteristics – my tendency to overthink things, my love for late-night coding sessions, my questionable coffee-making skills – will find their place among all the other brilliant minds here.
