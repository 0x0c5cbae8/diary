I’ve tried refactoring, rewriting entire sections, even commenting out chunks of code and rebuilding from scratch. Nothing. It’s always there, a phantom `1.0000000000000001` where a `1.0` should be, or a `-0.0000000000000001` instead of `0.0`. Enough to invalidate the data, but too small to be an obvious calculation error.

Maybe it’s the "Curse of the Freshman." The universe, or perhaps the Caltech faculty, testing my resilience.
