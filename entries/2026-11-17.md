Dear diary,

Another Tuesday, another blur of lectures, labs, and the looming shadow of PSETs. Phys 1a PSET 7 is due tomorrow, and I swear Gauss's Law is trying to personally haunt my dreams. Spent most of the afternoon wrestling with a particularly nasty integral, convinced for a solid hour that I'd fundamentally misunderstood electromagnetism. My brain feels like it's been wrung out and left to dry on a quantum clothesline.

I finally submitted it just before dinner, my fingers cramping and my eyes refusing to focus on anything smaller than a large font. I stumbled down to Chandler, utterly zombified. The main course was… well, it was Chandler. Edible, at least. But then, as I was about to head back to my room in Fleming and collapse, I saw it: little mini cheesecakes, perfectly portioned, sitting there on the dessert counter.

It was exactly what I needed. Seriously, the universe (or maybe just the dining staff) knew. I grabbed one, then, because it felt like a triumph, I grabbed a second. Sitting there, slowly spooning that creamy, sweet goodness, felt like a sacred ritual. Each bite was a tiny victory over the tyranny of vector fields and boundary conditions. It was a moment of pure, unadulterated pleasure. No PSETs, no deadlines, no impending midterms (okay, those are always impending, but I managed to ignore them for five glorious minutes). Just me, a surprisingly decent cheesecake, and the low hum of conversation from the other tables.

It's weird, but sometimes those small, simple things are what get you through the day here. A good cup of coffee, a quick chat with a housemate, or, tonight, a little indulgence that reminded me there's more to life than proofs and derivations. I'm still exhausted, but that little dessert ritual reset something in my brain. Maybe I *can* tackle that CS lab tonight after all. Or maybe I'll just sleep. Probably sleep. But first, a quick review of those PSET errors I'm sure I made.

Wish me luck for tomorrow.

-Trevor
