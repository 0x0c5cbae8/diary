Dear diary,

Another Sunday vanishes into the ether. I swear, the weekends here are less about rest and more about catching up on the rest of the work from the week, only to fall behind on *this* week's work. Just finished up the last touches on the PHYS 102 problem set – pretty sure I dreamt in vector fields last night.

Today had an unexpected deviation, though. My House decided it was time for some "pre-Thanksgiving festive ambiance," which translated to an afternoon of communal **decoration**. You'd think a bunch of engineers and scientists would approach it with some kind of optimized, efficient plan, but no. It was pure chaos. Someone brought a box of glitter, which I'm fairly certain will still be clinging to my hair follicles next semester. The debate over whether to use duct tape or masking tape for securing the paper turkeys to the common room wall escalated into a surprisingly passionate discussion about adhesive tensile strength. I ended up mostly untangling a perpetually knotted string of LED lights and trying to figure out if the circuit could handle another two strands without tripping the breaker. My main takeaway: holiday cheer is apparently directly proportional to fire hazard potential.

Honestly, it was a nice break, even with the glitter and the slightly illogical placement of a plastic pumpkin next to a theoretical physics poster. It felt… human. Less about solving for 'x' and more about just making things look a bit less like a sterile study lounge.

It actually got me thinking about my own room. I've been here for over two months now, and besides my textbooks and that mini-whiteboard for spontaneous derivations, there's practically no **decoration**. It's all functionality, no form. Seeing everyone else fuss over stringing up paper chains made me realize I hadn't even tacked up that cool poster from the JPL open house. It's still rolled up in the corner.

So, after dinner, I finally did it. Unrolled the poster – a beautiful, high-res shot of Jupiter from Juno – and carefully taped it above my desk. It's a small thing, really, just one picture. But the room already feels… softer. Less like a temporary containment unit for my brain, and more like *my* space. I even rearranged my desk lamp slightly so the light catches the poster just right. Might even look into some fairy lights next week, though I'll probably spend an hour researching their lumens per watt and expected lifespan before I buy them.

It's weird how a little bit of aesthetic effort can make such a difference, especially when you're surrounded by equations and coding all day. Maybe adding a few more 'decorative' elements isn't just a waste of time, but actually a necessary optimization for mental well-being.

Now, back to reading for MA 1a. At least I have Jupiter watching over me.

-Trevor
