Swimming around in this incredibly deep, complex pond, surrounded by what feel like full-grown, incredibly sophisticated frogs – the upperclassmen who seem to effortlessly churn out solutions, the grad students who talk about their research like it’s trivial, and the professors who are basically scientific deities.

Every day is like another step in a rapid, intellectual metamorphosis. One minute I’m trying to remember the difference between reduction and oxidation from high school chem, the next I’m grappling with quantum mechanics and the intricacies of frog leg development. My brain feels like it's trying to sprout limbs and reabsorb its tail all at once.

But then I remember why I’m here. To *become* a frog. To understand how those complex systems work, both in amphibians and in the universe. To contribute. Even if right now I just feel like a slightly stressed-out, oxygen-deprived polliwog trying to make sense of a PSet about myself, metaphorically speaking.
