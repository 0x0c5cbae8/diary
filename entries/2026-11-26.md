If the stillness outside is a barometer for campus population, then my internal stress level is a pretty reliable **barometer** for how close we are to finals week. And right now, it’s indicating "severe pressure warning." Each new derivation feels like a sudden drop in atmospheric pressure, leaving me feeling a bit lightheaded.

Mom called earlier, and I could practically hear her sigh when I told her I was "catching up on work." She meant well, asking if I was taking a *real* break. I chuckled. A Caltech "break" for a freshman like me mostly means swapping one set of intense academic pressures for another, slightly less immediate, but equally demanding, one. It’s a constant high-pressure system here, you learn to adapt to it.

I guess today's feast is a feast of differential equations and fluid dynamics. I just hope my mental barometer holds steady, or at least doesn't shatter, by the time the quarter officially ends. Time to dive back into this. Wish me luck.
